## Is L0201 the Monnaie du Moulin des Étuves? (2026-10-31)

The map marks **LE MOULIN DE LA MONNOIE** on the Seine just west of the Cité (L0201, 48.8574,
2.3429, approximate). Water-powered minting was being set up in Paris in exactly these years:
Henri II sent Guillaume de Marillac and Aubin Olivier to study the Augsburg screw press in **1550**,
and Olivier's *Monnaie du Moulin des Étuves*, at the **west end of the Île de la Cité**, was
definitively authorised in **July 1553** (fr.wikipedia, *Monnaie de Paris*).

If the mill on the map is that works, it bears on the map's date, which ch. 48 and ch. 53 of
*History of Cartography* already disagree about (1550 vs ca. 1553) — see the 2026-10-30 log entry.
**Nothing here establishes the identification**; the place's description says so. What would settle
it: where exactly the moulin des Étuves stood, and whether any mill of the Monnaie stood on that
spot before 1553.
