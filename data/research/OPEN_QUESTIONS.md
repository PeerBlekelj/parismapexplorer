## Research questions

Questions about the map's history rather than its labels. Each has its own note.

- **Where was the Chef Saint-Denis?** The publishers' shop "en la rue de Montorgueil, au chef Sainct-Denys". Narrowed to today's rue Montorgueil north of the rue Mauconseil, or the rue des Petits-Carreaux; the house is not located. Next steps: read Lepape 2016 and Grivel 2008/2015 in full; the archbishop's censiers (the 1575 one lists the Bon Pasteur) chained forward to the late-18th-century parcel plans Brette published; Minutier central deeds.
