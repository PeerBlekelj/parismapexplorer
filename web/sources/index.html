Sources

The books, records and plans this project reads from, in one place. Each place on the map is checked against them, and each link goes to the open copy.

In the library 12

These volumes are held in the project's research library, where they can be searched page by page. Only open sources are listed, and only their details: the text stays in the library. The list is rebuilt whenever something is added. The sources cited for individual claims are the references on the start page and the method page.

Guides to the city

  • La Fleur des antiquitez de la noble et triumphante ville et cité de Paris
    Gilles Corrozet · 1532 (éd. 1874) · 218 pages Gallica (BnF)

    Gilles Corrozet's first book on Paris, in the 1874 edition. It prints the poem the map later carries, with the acrostic that spells his name.

  • Les Antiquitez, histoires et singularitez de Paris, ville capitale du royaume de France ... (Par G. Corrozet.)
    Corrozet, Gilles (1510-1568) · 1550 · 459 pages Gallica (BnF)

    The first edition of Corrozet's guide, printed the year this project is set in.

  • Les antiquitez, histoires et singularitez excellentes de la ville, cité et université de Paris
    Gilles Corrozet · 1561 · 285 pages Gallica (BnF)

    The 1561 edition of the same guide, enlarged.
