Where you get at the starred list

The sidebar's top row is full at 380 px — search, Filters, and the Map / Table tabs — and tighter on a phone. Three places the way in could go. All three drive the same state, so they are not exclusive; the question is which one is always visible.

B · A chip in the Filters menu. Costs nothing to build: starred is just another filter next to the types and Unsure reading, and the Filters button already counts what is on. But it is two clicks and hidden behind a menu, so nobody who has not already found it will know their list is there. Worth having as well as A, not instead of it.

C · Its own button in the top row. The most conventional and the most obvious — but it has to take width from the search box, and the Table of places tab shortens to Table to make room. On a phone the row is already at its limit. Only worth it if the starred list is meant to be a headline feature rather than a convenience.
