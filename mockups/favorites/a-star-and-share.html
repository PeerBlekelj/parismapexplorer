Starring places, and sharing them

No accounts and no server: a star is kept in the visitor's own browser, and a link is how a place, a view or a whole list travels to someone else. The sidebar is the real one (web/src/style.css); the new parts are the two buttons on a card, the star on a row, the ★ in the count line, the bar under a starred list and the banner that a shared link opens with.
