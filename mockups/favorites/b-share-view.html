The map: sharing a view, and seeing your stars

Two things the map area gains. A ⤴ Share view button beside the orientation toggle, which copies a link to what you are looking at — the mode, where the cameras are, how far in, the overlay's opacity and the swipe. And starred places keep their ring in sepia gold at every zoom, so your own places stay findable while you move around. (The maps here are stand-ins.)

Share view. The link carries the state the app already keeps: …/map/?m=overlay&c=48.8529,2.3463&z=15.4&b=0&o=80&s=50 — mode, centre, zoom, bearing, old-map opacity, swipe position. Each part is optional, so an old link without one of them still opens. This is the piece that does not exist today: a place has a link, but “look at the overlay here” has none.
Starred rings. Ordinary dots only appear once you zoom past the point where labels stop colliding (OLD_DOTS_ZOOM, MOD_DOTS_ZOOM) or when a filter is on; starred ones ignore that rule and are drawn a size larger, in gold. They are the only pins on the map that are the visitor's own, so they should not look like the catalogue's red.
