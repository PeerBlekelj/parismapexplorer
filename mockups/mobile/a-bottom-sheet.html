A · Map with a bottom sheet

The map fills the screen; search, results and the place card live in a sheet that slides up from the bottom (Google Maps style). Drag or tap the grip: peek → half → full. The map always uses the space above the sheet, so a selected place stays in view. Two maps: stacked top/bottom (“Both”, the default, synced as on desktop), or Overlay / 1550 / Today in the same bar.
